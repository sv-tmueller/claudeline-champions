# ADR-005: original content

## Status

Accepted.

## Context

The game is in the spirit of Bloodline Champions. It must not reuse the
original game's content.

## Decision

Original content only: original champion names and kits inspired by the
archetypes, original arena layouts, no Stunlock names, art or text.

Alternative rejected: copying the original roster.

## Consequences

- Champion names, kits, arenas, art and text are written for this project.
- Archetypes may inspire kits. Names, art and text are never copied.
- Game design documents under `docs/design/` describe the original rules,
  roster and arenas.
