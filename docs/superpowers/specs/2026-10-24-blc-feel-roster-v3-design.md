# The Bloodline Champions feel: roster v3, the energy orb and self-cast

Status: design, written 2026-10-24 from a brainstorming session with the
project owner. It amends the approved design
([2026-10-01](2026-10-01-claudeline-champions-design.md)) in the places
section 8 lists, and it settles the six idea issues #158 to #163.

## 1. Intent

Claudeline Champions is a spiritual remake of Bloodline Champions (BLC,
Stunlock Studios, 2011). A match should play like BLC: two teams fight a
deathmatch in elimination rounds, the champions are balanced, and the
players' skill decides who wins a round, not an overpowered champion.

The owner's line between borrowing and copying:

- **Borrowed, as faithfully as we like:** the mechanics, the champion
  archetypes and the combat rhythm of BLC.
- **Our own, always:** every champion's name, look, colours, model and
  ability names, and the game's name and branding. The repo is public, and
  BLC's characters and names belong to Stunlock Studios. A champion here can
  play like Sentinel, but it is never called Sentinel and never looks like
  him.

This document adds the parts of the BLC feel the game does not have yet:

1. Four new mechanics in the effects toolkit (anti-heal, a fuse on areas, a
   projectile that leaves an area where it ends, an on-hit counter), each
   one arriving with the champion that uses it.
2. Three new champions, roster v3: a guardian tank, a bow archer and a star
   caster.
3. An energy orb: one contested pickup per round that charges the ultimate.
4. A self-cast control for support abilities.

## 2. Goals, non-goals, anti-goals

**Goals**

- Every new mechanic has a champion that uses it in real matches from the
  day the champion ships. No mechanic is built on its own and left unused.
- The tank ships first. It brings the counter to healing, and healing is the
  strongest thing in the game today: Lumeth wins 89.2 percent of the cells
  she is in (balance.md, the current report).
- Each champion is built in its own short series of batches, the way Ondrel
  and Brimwick went from design (batch 11) to kits and models (batch 12) to
  bots and client (batch 13).
- The match keeps its shape: elimination rounds, first to 3 round wins
  (about a best of five), one arena per match, fixed sides.

**Non-goals**

- No map rotation between rounds. The owner decided the arena stays the
  same for the whole match.
- No side switch between rounds, for now.
- No new mode. The respawn-entry idea (#163) waits for a respawn mode
  (section 7).
- No change to the six shipped kits, except what a balance batch later
  decides under its own rules.
- No new balance rule in this document. "Skill decides, not the champion"
  is the goal; the owner wants to look at how to measure it later. Until
  then the existing band applies (section 9.3).

**Anti-goals** (it would be a failure even if it technically works)

- A new champion that dominates its cells the way Lumeth does today.
- A mechanic that only the bots or only the tests ever exercise.
- Anything that reads as a copy of a BLC character: a name, a silhouette, a
  colour scheme or an ability name taken from BLC.
- A pickup that decides rounds on its own, so that whoever grabs it wins
  regardless of how the fight goes.

## 3. Constraints that carry over

These stay exactly as the approved design and the docs state them.

- **Determinism** (spec sections 4.3 and 4.8): integer ticks at 60 Hz,
  plain JSON state with entities in arrays in ascending id order, no `Map`
  or `Set`, no wall clock, no `Math.random`, no banned transcendental math
  (use `packages/sim/src/dmath.ts`). The checksum covers every new field
  automatically; optional keys are omitted, never set to `undefined`. Replay
  fixtures move only with the first diff named, under the keep rule.
- **The kit contract:** one JSON file per champion under `data/kits/`, all
  seven slots (`M1`, `M2`, `SPACE`, `Q`, `E`, `R`, `F`), exactly two EX
  variants and never one on `F`, EX cost 25 energy, `F` cost 100 with a
  cooldown of exactly 600 ticks. Max health 120 (ranged and support) to 160
  (melee) in steps of 10, move speed 5.5, damage and healing 8 to 30 per hit
  (up to 60 on `F`), projectile speed 12 to 25 m/s and radius 0.2 to 0.6 m.
  Every damaging slot (`M1`, `M2`, `Q`, `E`, `F`) carries damage, because
  only damage earns energy. The new-kit recipe is in
  `docs/architecture/testing.md` ("A new kit, the recipe").
- **The status catalog** (rules.md section 4.1) and the linger rules of
  roster-v2 section 0: a pulsing area totals 8 to 30 damage (8 to 60 on
  `F`), never carries stun, root or silence, and lingers for less than its
  slot's cooldown.
- **The package boundaries** in CLAUDE.md: the sim imports nothing, bots
  import the sim only, the client owns rendering and input.
- **Models:** each champion is generated by `tools/blender/gen_champion.py`
  on the shared body, rig and six clips, with its own `BODY_COLOURS` entry
  and cue builder. Colours must be distinct from the six shipped champions
  and from the HUD's reserved team colours (see roster-v2 decision 2).

## 4. The four new mechanics

Each mechanic is pure kit or arena data: no scripting. Each lands in the
sim with its validator rules, unit tests and a rules.md entry before the
kit that uses it, and no shipped kit uses it, so the shipped replay
fixtures and the balance block must not move when the mechanic merges.

### 4.1 Anti-heal, the `wound` status (#158, for the tank)

A new status, `wound`, that reduces the healing its holder receives.

- **What it reduces:** every `heal` effect and every `hot` pulse that lands
  on the holder. Shields are not healing and are not reduced.
- **Stacks, BLC style:** unlike slow, every application adds stacks,
  whoever applies it, the same caster included, up to a cap of 3 stacks.
  An application carries `stacks: 1..3` (default 1) and adds that many, so
  one hit that applies 3 stacks is one application. The holder keeps one
  stack count and one timer. On each application the timer becomes the
  longer of what is left and the new application's duration, so a short
  application never cuts a long one. When the timer runs out, all stacks go
  at once. This is a new stacking rule; the "same source refreshes and
  replaces" rule of rules.md section 4 does not apply to `wound`. (Lead's
  call, section 9.2.)
- **Strength:** 10 percent less healing per stack, so 30 percent at the cap
  `[tuned]`. The percent per stack is a catalog constant, not a per-kit
  field, so every source means the same thing.
- **Duration:** 120 to 300 ticks per application in the catalog `[tuned]`.
- **Invulnerable** blocks its application, like every hostile status.
- **Energy:** a reduced heal still pays support energy by the existing rule
  (it pays if it restores any health). No change to rules.md section 3.1.
- **Arithmetic:** the reduced amount is `amount * (100 - 10 * stacks) /
  100`, plain correctly-rounded operations.
- **Catalog row:** rules.md 4.1 gains the row and a per-status bullet;
  roster-v2's "an anti-heal" leaves the "not in the toolkit" list.

### 4.2 A fuse on areas (#159, for the star caster)

An optional `fuseTicks` field on the `area` effect: the number of ticks
from the moment the area is placed to its first pulse.

- **While armed** the area is visible and inert: it deals nothing and
  applies nothing. Then it behaves exactly like today's area for its
  `durationTicks`.
- **Range:** 15 to 120 ticks `[tuned]`. A fuse shorter than 15 ticks reads
  as instant and is not worth the telegraph.
- **Placement:** the area still reads the aim on the tick the cast fires
  (roster-v2 section 0) and stays where it was placed. The fuse does not
  follow anyone.
- **Round end** removes an armed area like every other area. Its pulses
  never fire into the round end pause.
- **The linger rule** counts the fuse: fuse plus lingering duration stays
  shorter than the slot's cooldown.
- **A fused one-hit area may carry stun, root or silence**, because the
  pulsing ban exists for repeated control, and a single delayed hit gives
  the target the fuse to walk out. A fused pulsing area keeps the ban.
- **The client** renders the armed state from the area entity (its spawn
  tick and fuse), with no new event kind.

### 4.3 A projectile that leaves an area where it ends (#160, for the star caster)

An optional `endArea` on the `projectile` effect: an area effect (the same
shape as today's `area`, with its `placement` ignored) that the projectile
spawns at its final position.

- **When it spawns:** when the projectile reaches the end of its
  `lifetimeTicks`, and when it stops on an obstacle with
  `blocksProjectiles: true` or on the arena edge.
- **When it does not:** when it hits a champion. The hit effects fire as
  today. Keeping the two disjoint keeps both testable and readable.
- **Pierce:** a projectile with `pierce` and an `endArea` is rejected by the
  validator.
- **Rules:** the spawned area obeys every area rule, the linger rules
  included, and counts as an area of the projectile's slot.
- **The client** renders the transition from the existing events
  (projectile despawn, area spawn), with no new event kind.

### 4.4 The on-hit counter (#161, for the bow archer)

A per-kit counter that one slot's hits build and other abilities spend.
Roster-v1 says a kit has no passive; this is the one exception, and it is
declared in the kit data, not hidden in code.

- **Declaration** (on the kit): `counter: { from: <slot>, cap: <1..5>,
  windowTicks: <60..600> }`. Only one counter per kit.
- **Build:** a hit by the `from` slot that lands on an enemy adds one, up to
  the cap. A hit on an invulnerable champion adds nothing.
- **Decay:** the whole counter resets to 0 when `windowTicks` pass with no
  qualifying hit. One timer per champion, not one per stack.
- **Round reset:** the counter resets to 0 at the start of each round, like
  energy.
- **State:** two plain numbers on the champion (the count and the tick of
  the last qualifying hit), omitted when a kit has no counter.
- **Spend:** an ability carries a top-level effect `consumeCounter` with one
  of two verbs. It spends the whole counter on the tick the cast fires, and
  does nothing when the counter is 0.
  - `{ bonusDamagePerStack: <n> }`: the damage effects of this cast deal `n`
    more per stack spent. The damage range applies to the total.
  - `{ cooldownTicksPerStack: <n>, slot: <slot> }`: the named slot's
    current cooldown is reduced by `n` ticks per stack spent, never below 0.
- **The client** shows the count as pips under the archer's own health bar
  and in the HUD (a small number of pips, no new screen).

This splits into two packages: 4.4a the counter (declaration, build, decay,
reset, state) and 4.4b the spend verbs. 4.4b depends on 4.4a.

## 5. Roster v3: three new champions

The names and ids are this document's proposal. The numbers are drafts
(`[draft]`): each champion's design package (section 10, step 1 of its
series) fixes them in `docs/design/roster-v3.md` against the catalog, the
same way roster-v2 fixed Ondrel's and Brimwick's.

The gaps the six leave, which the three fill:

- **No tank.** Hargrim trades damage; nobody protects allies or holds a
  line, and nothing counters healing.
- **No stack-building marksman.** Quillon is a skillshot duellist; nothing
  rewards keeping up sustained hits.
- **No delayed area caster.** Every area fires the tick its cast ends, so
  nobody has to predict where an enemy will be.

**Fantasy.** A heavy shield-and-hammer warden who walks into the enemy
healer and makes every heal worth less. His own name, look and colours.

| M2 | Sundering Blow | Area 2 m ahead, radius 2 m, one hit: 16 damage and 1 `wound` stack. Windup 0.4 s, slowed. Cooldown 5 s. **EX:** 22 damage and 2 stacks. |
| SPACE | Bulwark Charge | Dash 5 m; at the end a one-hit self area, radius 1.5 m: knockback 2 m away. Cooldown 8 s. |
| Q | Grounding Slam | Self area, radius 2.5 m, one hit: 10 damage and root 1 s. Cooldown 10 s. |
| E | Breaker Throw | Projectile, speed 18, range 9 m: 16 damage, 1 `wound` stack and slow 30 percent for 1.5 s. Cooldown 7 s. **EX:** 22 damage and 2 stacks. |
| R | Aegis Line | Self area, radius 4 m, targets allies (him included): shield 25 for 4 s. Cooldown 14 s. |
| F | Last Bastion | Self area, radius 4.5 m, one hit: 30 damage, 3 `wound` stacks and slow 50 percent for 2 s; he gains a 50 shield for 4 s. Windup 0.5 s, locked. |

**Counter web (intent).** Beats Lumeth (`wound` on her targets), Skerrow

## 6. The energy orb

One contested pickup per round that charges the ultimate. It rewards map
control without deciding the round.

- **Where:** one orb point per arena, kept out of the arena files.
  arenas.md:94 forbids changing arena01, because every sim fixture
  checksums `state.arena`, so the points live in a separate table keyed by
  arena id (for example `data/arenas/orbs.json`). A point must lie on the
  mirror axis (x = 0) so neither team is closer, inside the ring's end
  circle so it stays contested in sudden death, and on walkable floor.
  Arena 1 has a pillar at the centre, so its point sits beside the pillar
  on the axis; arena 2 uses the centre. An arena with no row has no orb.
- **Switched by the match:** `MatchConfig.energyOrb` (optional, off when
  omitted) turns the orb on. The standard match the client starts and the
  online server set it; the committed replay fixtures and the existing
  tests do not, so they keep their checksums. The orb package adds its own
  fixture with the orb on. (Lead's call, section 9.2.)
- **Balance with the orb:** the orb changes every match after 20 s, so the
  orb series' second batch reruns `npm run balance` with the orb on, records
  the moved block under the keep rule, and from then on the harness plays
  the standard match, orb included.
- **When:** a warning appears 17 s into the round (a visible marker and a
  sound), and the orb becomes collectible at 20 s `[tuned]`.
- **Pickup:** the first living champion whose circle touches the orb (orb
  radius 0.5 m) takes it. It gains 25 energy, capped at 100, and the orb is
  gone for the rest of the round. Unclaimed, it stays until the round ends.
- **Ties:** when champions of both teams touch it on the same tick, the
  match's seeded RNG picks one. Never the lowest id, which would favour a
  team.
- **Events:** an `orbSpawn` warning and an `orbTaken` event (who took it),
  for the client and the kill feed.
- **Free play:** no orb (no round clock).
- **Bots:** a bot contests the orb when it is up, no enemy is in its threat
  range, and it is closer than any enemy; otherwise it ignores it. Bot
  profiles need no per-champion rule for this.
- **The client** draws the warning marker, the orb, and a line in the kill
  feed when it is taken.

## 7. Self-cast (#162) and respawn entry (#163)

### 7.1 Self-cast: hold C

Holding **C** while casting a slot places the aim on the caster's own
position for that cast, the way BLC's self-cast landed a heal on yourself.
The input record does not change (no new button bit), so the replay format
and the online protocol are untouched. It has two parts: the client key,
and one sim rule that makes an aim on yourself mean something.

**The client key.** While C is held, the client sets the aim to the
caster's own position on every tick, so the aim is still on the caster on
the tick the windup ends and the cast fires (rules.md:116 reads the aim
then). Releasing C before the cast fires cancels the self-cast; the aim
goes back to the cursor.

**The sim rule: a self-aimed ally projectile lands on the caster.**
(The owner's decision.)

- **When:** a cast fires a projectile that has `allyEffects`, and the aim
  on that tick lies within the caster's own circle (0.5 m, the champion
  radius). Aim is in whole centimetres, which is precise enough for this.
- **What happens:** the projectile does not spawn. Its `allyEffects` apply
  to the caster at once, on that tick. Its enemy `effects` do nothing,
  because no projectile flies.
- **The self-heal penalty:** a `heal` and every pulse of a `hot` applied
  this way give 60 percent of their amount `[tuned]`. Shields and other
  ally statuses apply in full. (The owner asked for a weaker heal on
  yourself; 60 percent is the lead's call, section 9.2.)
- **Energy:** unchanged. Healing yourself already gives no energy
  (rules.md section 3.1).
- **`wound`** applies on top: a wounded healer's self-heal is cut by both,
  multiplied (60 percent of the wound-reduced amount).
- **A mouse on your own champion** also triggers the rule, with or without
  C, because the sim only sees the aim. That is intended: C is the reliable
  way to aim there under pressure, not the only way.
- **Bots** do not self-cast in this design. A later bots package may add
  it, and its balance run shows the effect.
- **Fixtures:** the rule changes a cast only when the aim falls inside the
  caster's circle. If a committed fixture ever recorded such an aim, it
  moves under the keep rule with the first diff named.

**The rest of the control:**

- **Why C:** modifier keys collide with the browser and the OS. Ctrl+W
  closes the tab and W is "move up"; Alt+Space opens the Windows system
  menu and Space is the dash. A held letter key is safe everywhere. C sits
  under the movement keys and is unused.
- **Rebindable:** C joins the rebindable defaults in `bindings.ts` as
  `selfCast: 'KeyC'`. Shift still selects the EX variant, so C plus Shift
  plus a slot self-casts the EX.
- **What it reaches:** every ally projectile, by the sim rule above, so
  Lumeth's Mend Bolt and Soothing Surge can land on Lumeth herself. Effects
  cast around the caster (`placement: self`) already include the caster
  and are unaffected. An aimed enemy ability cast with C lands at the
  caster's feet, which is the player's choice.
- **rules.md section 6** gains the binding beside the existing defaults,
  and rules.md gains the self-aim rule and the 60 percent penalty.
- **Two packages,** independent of each other: the sim rule, and the
  client key. They can share a batch.

### 7.2 Respawn entry: deferred

The respawn-entry choice (BLC's Capture the Artifact teleporters) only
matters in a mode where champions die and respawn apart from the round
cycle. The elimination format has no respawn, and modes beyond 2v2, 3v3
and a later 1v1 are milestone 9's "additional modes". Recommendation:
defer to that mode's own design. #163 closes with this section as its
record.

## 9. What stays open, and who settles it

### 9.1 Left to the builder

- Exact numbers within the catalog, fixed per champion in
  `docs/design/roster-v3.md` (the `[draft]` values above are the starting
  point, not a contract).
- Champion colours, models and cue builders, within section 3's colour
  rule.
- The orb's look and sound, and the exact orb point on arena 1.
- Field names in the kit and arena schemas where this document gives only
  the meaning.
- The bots' rules for each new champion (openers, escapes, `holdF`), as the
  existing profiles do it.

### 9.2 Decided here as the lead's call, open to the owner's veto

- The names Durnhal, Wrenna and Ysmeer, and their ability names.
- The C key for self-cast; the self-heal penalty at 60 percent; bots not
  self-casting yet. (That a self-aimed ally projectile lands on the caster
  with a weaker heal is the owner's decision.)
- The orb numbers: 25 energy, a warning at 17 s, collectible at 20 s.
- The orb kept out of the arena files and switched by
  `MatchConfig.energyOrb`, so the shipped fixtures keep their checksums.
- `wound` at 10 percent per stack and a cap of 3; an application carries a
  stack count, and the timer takes the longer of what is left and the new
  duration.
- The balance plan: the six-kit report first, each new champion joins the
  harness, the runtime grows.
- Amending the "six until online" cap.

### 9.3 Balance

Today the balance harness measures only four kits
(`HARNESS_KITS` in `tools/balance/inputs.ts:11`); Ondrel and Brimwick wait
for the six-kit report, which is planned after lanes A and B. So
(lead's calls, section 9.2):

- **The six-kit report comes first.** It is a prerequisite of the tank
  series' third batch, and it ships in the batch after lanes A and B if it
  is not already done.
- **Each new champion joins the harness** in its series' last batch and is
  measured against every champion in it, on both modes and both arenas, in
  the 40 to 60 percent band per cell (docs/design/balance.md).
- **Runtime grows with the roster:** 9 kits make 36 pairings, 144 cells
  against today's 24. The local run grows to roughly 30 to 35 minutes, and
  the manual CI workflow (`.github/workflows/balance.yml`) to well over an
  hour. Both are acceptable for a manual check. If the CI workflow hits its
  time limit, the package that adds the kit may lower the default `--seeds`
  or split the run by mode, and says so in the report.
- A champion outside the band gets a tuning package in the next batch, as
  the roster-v1 passes did.

The owner will decide later how to measure "skill decides".

## 10. Build order

Each series is its own batches, and every batch keeps the batch rules (up
to six packages, no dependency inside a batch). Lanes A and B (batches 14A
and 14B: the bots profiles, the end-ring stall, the server skeleton, the
client net core, the roster to six) run first, in their own sessions.

1. **The tank series**
   1. Durnhal's section of roster-v3.md (docs), and `wound` in the sim
      (4.1). Self-cast (7.1) fits here too, as two independent packages:
      the sim rule and the client key.
   2. Durnhal's kit as data, and his model.
   3. Durnhal's bot profile, his client roster entry, and the balance check
      (after the six-kit report, section 9.3).
2. **The orb series**
